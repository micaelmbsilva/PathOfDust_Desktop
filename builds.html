Calculation boundary

Tier-119 gear, maximum ordinary affix rolls and polish, full durability, and neutral boss power/jitter. No fifth modifiers, Split Personality, Sacred, Perfect, Celestial or unique affixes, Krangled items, or other rare-drop states. These are achievable ceilings, not average-drop expectations.
